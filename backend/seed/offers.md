# Current Offers & Promotions

## Are there any offers or discounts right now?
Yes. Our current promotions are:

- **New Customer 10% Off** — use code **WELCOME10** at checkout on your first order.
- **Seasonal Sale** — up to **30% off** selected styles in the Sale collection.
- **Free Shipping** — on all orders over **$75** (no code needed; applied automatically).

Offers may change; the live discount shown at checkout always takes precedence.

## Do you have any bundles?
Yes, we offer curated bundles at a lower combined price than buying each item separately:

- **Complete the Look Bundle** — any top + bottom together for **15% off** the pair.
- **Starter Wardrobe Bundle** — 3 essentials (a shirt, a pair of trousers, and a jacket) for a fixed bundle price.
- **Buy 2 Get 1 Free** — on socks, basics, and accessories.

Bundle discounts are applied automatically in the cart when qualifying items are added.

## How do I use a discount code?
Add your items to the cart, go to checkout, and enter the code in the **"Discount code"** box, then select **Apply**. Only one discount code can be used per order, but automatic offers (like free shipping and bundle pricing) still stack with a code unless stated otherwise.

## When does the sale end?
Seasonal sale periods vary. The Sale collection page shows the items currently on offer and any end date. Promo codes show their expiry at checkout.

## Are there student or first-responder discounts?
We don't currently run a dedicated student or first-responder program. The best value is the **WELCOME10** first-order code and our seasonal Sale collection.

## Can I get a price adjustment if an item goes on sale after I buy it?
If an item you bought goes on sale within **7 days** of your purchase, contact support with your order number and we'll review a price adjustment to the sale price.
