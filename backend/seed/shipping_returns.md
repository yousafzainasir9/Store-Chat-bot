# Shipping

## How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United States. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders are processed within 1 business day of being placed.

## How much does shipping cost?
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75. For orders under $75, standard shipping is a flat $6.95. Express shipping is $14.95.

## Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. International shipping takes 7 to 14 business days. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by their country.

# Returns and exchanges

## What is your return policy?
We accept returns of unworn, unwashed items with tags attached within 30 days of delivery. Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days of us receiving the return.

## How do I start a return or exchange?
Visit the Returns Center linked in your order confirmation email, enter your order number and email, and select the items you wish to return or exchange. You will receive a prepaid return label.

## Is return shipping free?
Return shipping is free for exchanges. For refunds, a $5.95 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund unless the item was damaged or incorrect.

## Can I exchange an item for a different size?
Yes. Start an exchange in the Returns Center and select the new size. If the new size is in stock it ships immediately; exchanges do not incur a return shipping fee.
