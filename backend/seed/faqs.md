# Account and ordering

## How do I create an account?
Click "Sign in" at the top right of any page, then choose "Create account". You only need an email address and a password. You can also check out as a guest without an account.

## What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay. All payments are processed securely; we never store your full card number.

## Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 60 minutes of being placed, as long as they have not yet entered fulfillment. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number and we will do our best to help.

## Do you offer gift cards?
Yes. Digital gift cards are available in denominations from $25 to $250 and are delivered by email. Gift cards never expire and can be used on any item.

## How do I use a discount code?
Enter your discount code in the "Discount" box at checkout and click "Apply". Only one discount code can be used per order, and codes cannot be combined with other promotions.
